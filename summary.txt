Autobiography Publishing helps you turn your life story into a finished book, handling the editing, design, printing, and distribution while you stay in charge of the pen. Your story is a gift to your family and future generations: a way to preserve memories, pass on lessons, and share the people, places, and punchlines that made you who you are. You keep all rights to your book, choose who reads it, from family-only to the wider world, and can update, correct, or remove content whenever you like.

Getting published takes five easy steps. You submit your manuscript (every autobiography submission is reviewed free of charge), get approved, and are paired with a personal coordinator who guides you with patience and respect for your privacy. The team covers copyediting, proofreading, typesetting, eBook conversion, cover design, and marketing, with nothing moving forward without your okay. Your materials are protected with security safeguards, and once you're published, the company manages distribution and royalties while you enjoy the title of author.
